Title

Military-Themed Chess Set

Sheet 08 of 08
Dates
Feb 2025 – Mar 2025
Context
SolidWorks Assignment 3, "Chessboard with Style": an individual assignment in Cornerstone Engineering 2 (GE 1502)
Role
Individual: theme, all six piece designs, the assembly, and the documentation
Tools
SolidWorks (parts, assembly, and standard views)

Designed a Military Warfare chess set in SolidWorks, with six pieces each matched to how that piece moves, and assembled a full side on the board across three weekly submissions.

Key numbers

Piece designs
6
Pieces assembled
16
Weekly submissions
3
Common base: diameter, draft
80 mm, 70°

Objective and constraints

The brief called for:

  • six themed pieces on a common base built to the assignment's specification
  • every required technique used at least once: 3-point arc, spline, polygon, mirror entities, loft, revolved boss/base, fillet, draft, and a shell with one open side
  • a height order of King, Queen, Bishop, Knight, Rook, Pawn
  • one full side set up on the provided board with proper mates

Pieces and why

King: HQ command bunker
Vital and heavily fortified but barely mobile, like the king.
Queen: Gatling gun
Rapid fire in any direction, like the queen's sweeping range. My favorite piece.
Bishop: sniper tower
Long sight lines from a fixed position, like the bishop's diagonals.
Knight: mortar
Indirect, arcing fire over obstacles, like the knight's L-shaped jump.
Rook: M202 FLASH rocket launcher
Straight-line force along ranks and files.
Pawns: missiles
Expendable and forward-only, but powerful when promoted.

What I did

  • Started with a fighter-jet theme in Part 1 (an F-16 king, a B-2 queen, and missile pawns), then switched to Military Warfare in Part 2 while keeping the level of detail I wanted.
  • Built every piece on the assignment's common base: an 80 mm circle lofted to a hexagon on a 35 mm reference plane, with a 15° reference plane and a 70° draft.
  • Used a wide range of SolidWorks features: revolve, loft, shell, wrap, fill pattern, split line, offset surface, thicken, flatten, draft, circular and linear patterns, reference planes, fillets, and chamfers.
  • Modeled the pieces to 0.5 mm tolerances.
  • Assembled one full side (16 pieces) on the provided board, fixing the board first and mating each piece with concentric and coincident mates.
  • Documented the final board in standard views (isometric, front, and side).

Result

Completed all six piece designs and the assembled board across the three submissions.
