I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at Northeastern University, drawn to automation, design, and manufacturing: the processes that turn an idea into a physical part. It started when I repaired a broken RC car as a kid by reconnecting its servo wires, and I've been taking things apart and building them ever since.

I grew up in Mumbai, where I interned as a test engineer at a BMW workshop, built a voice-controlled chess robot at the Makers Asylum residency, and founded an e-waste recycling initiative. At Northeastern, I'm on the Baja SAE team and work on design projects like the Beach Bot's lift arm. I do my best work on fast-paced, multidisciplinary teams.

Outside engineering, I'm learning Japanese, I love to travel, and I'm always up for adventure sports.
